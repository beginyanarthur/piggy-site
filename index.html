Piggy · iPhone · Beta

Money in bags, month by month

Every bit of money you have goes into a bag: rent, food, fun, a trip, savings. When money comes in you split it between your bags. When you spend, you take it from the bag it belongs to.

What it does

  • Give every dram a place. Make a bag for anything you spend on or put aside for, and split new income between them in one screen.
  • Log a spend in seconds. The round button on any screen: pick a bag, type the amount.
  • Plan ahead. Decide next month before it arrives, and fund it as your income comes in.
  • Check your money. Count the cash and cards you really have, and see whether Piggy agrees.
  • Know what you owe. Start a bag with a debt on it and watch it climb back to zero.

Your data stays with you

Your budget lives on your phone. No bank connection, no ads, and no account on a server. Piggy counts anonymous events, such as "a bag was made", to see which parts of the app get used. No amounts, no bag names, no notes, and nothing that identifies you. You can turn it off in Settings.
